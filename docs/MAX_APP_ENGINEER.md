# Omnia MAX App Engineer

## Purpose

The MAX App Engineer is the specialised product identity of Omnia's existing
native code agent. It does not fork a second generator and is not a larger prompt
or a bundle of design plugins. It combines deterministic pre-code decisions,
safe project tools, a maintained MAX runtime and executable release gates in one
observable loop.

## Lifecycle

1. The shared discovery and BuildPlan establish screens, data and user outcomes.
2. `max_design_director.py` compiles and persists `max_app_engineer` inside the
   project's `discovery_spec` before any model-authored product write.
3. The DesignDNA records three different concepts and one justified selection,
   plus the audience, promise, composition, type, semantic colour, geometry,
   density, chart, motion, signature interaction and blacklist contract.
4. Capability planning selects only relevant managed AI, payment, lead, catalogue,
   analytics and persistence contracts; MAX identity/Bridge and legal are always
   present. Every contract names truthful unavailable/error states and proof.
5. The existing native loop reads the mandatory premium mobile foundation and
   relevant domain slice, writes within MAX's path/secret/API/DB boundaries,
   compiles, runs and inspects the signed preview.
6. The independent visual judge receives both the business brief and selected
   DesignDNA. It assesses 360px and 390px renders for hierarchy, character,
   legibility, density, brief fidelity, interaction truth, craft and adaptation.
7. `broken`, `generic`, a score below 8 or missing proof is red. The bounded loop
   permits at most two evidence-led repair passes; unresolved output is not
   published.
8. The final release proof re-runs build, runtime, hydration, signed navigation,
   primary/persisted actions and reload restoration. Design, capabilities and the
   360/390 visual verdict are folded into the same tamper-evident attestation and
   snapshot transaction.

Studio receives the Design Director and every subsequent action through the
existing `agent.step` event shape, so no parallel progress protocol or large UI
surface is required.

## Why quality becomes reliable

Plugins can add references, imagery or telemetry, but cannot decide the product's
art direction or prove that an action works. Reliability comes from four layers:

- a deterministic decision survives retries and prevents aesthetic drift;
- three composition/type/geometry/motion concepts prevent colour-only variation;
- accessible primitives and domain skills give the model strong reusable craft
  without forcing a recognisable template;
- independent screenshots and functional gates reject generic polish, fake data,
  fake success and missing evidence before release.

The model still has broad visual authorship. The platform owns the decision
record, truth boundaries and acceptance floor.
