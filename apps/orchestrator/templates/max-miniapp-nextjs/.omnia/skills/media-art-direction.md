# Product media art direction

Generated media is justified when it carries product meaning, atmosphere or
orientation that typography and interface geometry cannot carry alone.

- Decide the media's job first: establish context, visualise a goal/state, teach a
  movement, distinguish a category, or create one emotional anchor.
- Direct one coherent system—camera, lighting, palette, material, subject distance,
  crop behaviour and negative space—not a collection of unrelated AI pictures.
- Compose for the actual mobile slot and safe crop. Keep important content away
  from text and controls; provide a legible fallback/background while loading.
- Prefer one strong generated asset reused intelligently over per-card generation.
  Do not turn utility screens into a poster or use media to hide weak hierarchy.
- Generated video must be short, muted/playsInline, optional and performance-aware.
  Provide a still fallback and respect reduced-motion/data constraints.
- Never generate fake screenshots, unreadable text inside imagery, deceptive
  evidence, or people/claims that imply a real endorsement.
- After generation, embed the returned hosted URL into source and verify the
  running route. A successful media tool call without product integration is not
  completion.

If media does not improve the primary action or product character, skip it and
invest the budget in interaction quality.
