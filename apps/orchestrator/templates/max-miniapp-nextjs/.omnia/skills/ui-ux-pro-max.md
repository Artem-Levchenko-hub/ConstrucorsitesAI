# ui-ux-pro-max plugin bridge

This capability exposes a compact project-matched slice of Omnia's vendored
`ui-ux-pro-max` knowledge base. The executor appends candidates from its colour,
typography, visual-language, compatible-icon, chart and high-severity UX datasets
below. Recommendations that require a dependency absent from the MAX starter are
discarded by the executor.

These candidates are evidence, not a selected preset. Compare them inside the
three-direction exploration, adapt them to MAX/mobile constraints, and reject
anything that conflicts with the user's product, content or chosen character.
Never copy a landing-page structure into a MAX Mini App.
