# Art Direction

This is not a template. Build a visual thesis for this product, not a decorated
version of a familiar dashboard.

## Three-direction exploration

Before committing visual code, describe three directions that differ in
composition, typographic voice, geometry, density, colour roles, imagery and
motion grammar. Colour-only variations do not count. Reject the safest generic
direction unless it is uniquely right for the audience. Choose one and state
why it makes the primary action clearer, more trusted or more desirable.

## Build a coherent character

- Treat type as structure. Use one expressive decision and a disciplined body
  system; protect Cyrillic quality, numerical alignment and mobile wraps.
- Compose with rhythm, tension and breathing room. Repetition establishes a
  system; contrast reveals priority.
- Define semantic colours before component colours. Confirm readable foreground
  pairs and do not make the accent carry every meaning.
- Establish one geometry logic for radii, borders, icons and elevation. Nested
  surfaces should feel optically related, not generated independently.
- Use imagery only when it explains, motivates or establishes real identity.
  Direct crop, light, palette and subject consistently.

## Originality guard

Start from the product's meaningful object and action, never from a hero, card
grid, stats dashboard or bottom-nav recipe. Remove any section that could be
pasted unchanged into an unrelated app. One memorable, useful signature is
better than ornamental novelty across every screen.

## Render critique

At 390px, inspect hierarchy, line breaks, density, touch confidence and the
relationship between content and chrome. If the app feels generic, strengthen
the thesis, composition or domain object instead of adding decoration.
