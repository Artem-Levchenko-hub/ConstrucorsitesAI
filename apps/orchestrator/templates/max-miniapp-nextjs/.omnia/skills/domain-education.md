# Domain Intelligence — Education

This is not a template. Design for comprehension and momentum, not a catalogue
of colourful lessons.

## Domain objects and loop

Programme, module, lesson, activity, attempt, feedback and mastery are different
objects. The learning loop is orient → attempt → receive specific feedback →
reflect → choose the next useful challenge.

- Show why a lesson matters and what the learner will be able to do.
- Progress reflects demonstrated work, never page views alone.
- Give feedback close to the attempt and explain how to improve; do not expose
  an answer before meaningful effort when the learning goal requires recall.
- Resume precisely after interruption and preserve drafts or attempts.
- Separate locked prerequisites, optional enrichment and completed work.

Use honest empty states for a new learner. Saved attempts and progress come from
authenticated persistence, not seeded achievements. AI tutoring should adapt to
context, show uncertainty, avoid pretending to grade unsupported work and offer
a route to retry. Minor/student data or public content triggers `trust-safety`.

Activation is the first completed learning activity with feedback; retention is
meaningful continuation, not notification volume.
