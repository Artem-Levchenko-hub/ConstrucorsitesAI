# MAX capability catalog

These are optional specialist lenses, not templates. On a fresh build, identify
the one to three capabilities that are genuinely hard or distinctive in the
brief and call `read_skill` with their exact slug. Do not load every pack. For a
surgical edit, skip skill loading unless the change crosses a specialist domain.

- `product-strategy` — turn an ambiguous idea into a focused mobile product,
  task flow, information architecture and meaningful states.
- `mobile-motion` — native-feeling interaction choreography, spatial continuity,
  haptics, performance and reduced-motion behaviour.
- `data-storytelling` — select truthful mobile data representations and make
  dense analytics understandable without a desktop dashboard.
- `ai-native-ux` — design real Google AI interactions with useful context,
  uncertainty, recovery, feedback and trust.
- `max-platform` — use MAX identity, Bridge and managed Omnia integrations as
  product capabilities without hallucinating platform APIs.
- `quality-accessibility` — mobile accessibility, touch ergonomics, semantic
  structure, contrast, focus, localization and resilient edge states.
- `media-art-direction` — decide when generated imagery materially improves a
  product and direct it as one coherent visual system.
- `ui-ux-pro-max` — query Omnia's vendored ui-ux-pro-max knowledge for
  project-matched colour, typography, visual-language, icon, chart and UX
  evidence. The returned candidates are raw material, never a chosen preset.

Skill packs may disagree. Reconcile them against the user's primary action and
the selected art direction; the brief always wins.
