# MAX capability catalog

These are decision lenses, not templates. Use `read_skill` at the moment a lens
can change a decision; do not front-load every pack or reload a pack already used
in the run. Surgical edits inherit the established product and visual system.

## Lifecycle core

- `ui-ux-pro-max` — master creative router and project-matched design evidence.
- `product-flow` — scenarios, information architecture, onboarding and the
  shortest honest path to activation. Read before the first product write.
- `art-direction` — three genuinely different concepts, typography,
  composition and visual character. Read before committing visual code.
- `production-readiness` — real MAX account bootstrap, user-scoped persistence,
  truthful empty states and no test data in production. Read before product code.
- `visual-evaluation` — independent screenshot critique for quality,
  functionality and brief fidelity. Read after the first rendered `see` and
  before `done` on a full build.

## Trigger only when the brief needs it

- `interaction-motion` — state choreography, bottom sheets, transitions,
  haptics, interruption and reduced motion.
- Load exactly one matching domain pack: `domain-fitness`, `domain-restaurant`,
  `domain-booking`, `domain-education` or `domain-commerce`.
- `trust-safety` — payments, user content, personal, health or medical data.
- `growth-analytics` — activation events, funnels, retention and experiments.

The lifecycle core establishes coherence and a usable published product. Triggered packs add domain depth
without changing the brief or imposing a reusable screen skeleton.

- `product-strategy` — turn an ambiguous idea into a focused mobile product,
  task flow, information architecture and meaningful states.
- `mobile-motion` — native-feeling interaction choreography, spatial continuity,
  haptics, performance and reduced-motion behaviour.
- `data-storytelling` — select truthful mobile data representations and make
  dense analytics understandable without a desktop dashboard.
- `ai-native-ux` — design real Google AI interactions with useful context,
  uncertainty, recovery, feedback and trust.
- `max-platform` — use MAX identity, Bridge and managed Omnia integrations as
  product capabilities without hallucinating platform APIs.
- `quality-accessibility` — mobile accessibility, touch ergonomics, semantic
  structure, contrast, focus, localization and resilient edge states.
- `media-art-direction` — decide when generated imagery materially improves a
  product and direct it as one coherent visual system.
- `ui-ux-pro-max` — the mandatory first-build creative-virtuoso lens: product
  emotion, three-direction art exploration, signature interaction, delight map,
  mobile craft and project-matched colour/type/pattern evidence. It expands
  creative range without choosing a preset or reusable visual skeleton.

Skill packs may disagree. Reconcile them against the user's primary action and
the selected art direction; the brief always wins.
